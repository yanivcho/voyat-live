Voyat

Everything you need, on the way

What it does

Voyat is a personal travel assistant for Android. Instead of asking you to search for what you need, it works out which stage of the trip you are in and puts the right thing in front of you — when to pack, when to check in, when your passport is getting close to expiring, and what to do in an emergency abroad.

Main features

  • Smart timeline — reminders that follow the stage of your trip.
  • Offline wallet — your travel documents and their expiry dates, available with no internet connection.
  • International emergency screen — real emergency numbers for 44 countries, chosen by the country you are actually in, and working offline.
  • Flight compensation — checks EU261 and Israeli law and takes the better of the two.
  • Import from a booking email — share a confirmation email and the flight, hotel, insurance or car details are filled in for you.
  • Shared trips — everyone travelling together sees the same trip.

Privacy, briefly

Passport, national ID and driving licence numbers are never stored. Photos of your documents stay on your device. There is no advertising and no tracking. The full details are in the privacy policy.
