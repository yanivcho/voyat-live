Voyat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

What Voyat is

Voyat is a personal travel assistant. It keeps your trip details, travel documents and reminders in one place, and works offline while you are abroad.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and display name.
  • Trip data you enter: destination, dates, itinerary stops, packing list, budget and expenses, emergency contacts.
  • Documents you add: document type, label, expiry date and holder name.

What we never store

  • Passport numbers, national ID numbers and driving licence numbers are removed before anything is saved — on the device and on the server alike.
  • Photos of your documents stay on your device only. They are never uploaded to our servers and are not part of any sync or backup we hold.

Location

Voyat reads your location only when you actively ask it to: when you send your location from the emergency screen, or when you start live route sharing.

  • Live sharing always has an expiry — a maximum of 8 hours — and the stored location is deleted when you stop sharing or when it expires.
  • The emergency screen also uses your approximate location offline, on the device itself, to show the right emergency numbers for the country you are in.
  • Voyat does not track your location in the background for any other purpose.

Email booking confirmations

Voyat can read a booking confirmation to fill in your trip. It reads only the single email you explicitly share with the app. Voyat does not open, search or monitor your mailbox.

Where the data is stored

Account and trip data are stored with Supabase on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU), and are protected by row-level security so that each account can read only its own data. A copy is kept on your device so the app works without an internet connection.

What we do not do

  • No advertising.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking software.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Deleting your data

You can delete any document, delete a trip, or start over from within the app. To have your account and all of its server-side data deleted, email the address below and it will be removed.
